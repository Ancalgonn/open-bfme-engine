# Community code of conduct

OpenBFME is a technical collaboration space. Treat contributors, players,
modders, and maintainers with respect regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give reproducible evidence, accept correction, and make room for newcomers.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and the project's retail-content boundary.
- Follow maintainer and moderator directions during incidents.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
impersonation, spam, scams, malicious files, or deliberate distribution of
private or copyrighted game content are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report ordinary community problems to a maintainer or Discord moderator. Report
security vulnerabilities through GitHub private security reporting. Do not put
credentials, exploit details, retail payloads, or personal information in a
public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the project and its community. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and free of conflicts
of interest where practical.
